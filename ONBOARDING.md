# Getting Started with Your Vault

Your vault is a living system. It starts with four files and grows into something no competitor can replicate — a complete map of your expertise that generates content, handles objections, writes proposals, and thinks like you.

This guide walks you through the first steps.

---

## The Four Files

Everything starts here. These are the foundation of your vault:

| File | What it captures | Why it matters |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Soul** | Your core beliefs, origin story, what makes you different | Every output reflects your identity, not generic advice |
| **Audience** | Who buys, what they struggle with, their exact words | Content speaks to real problems in language buyers recognise |
| **Offer** | The transformation you deliver, why it's worth paying for | Proposals, ads, and emails explain your value clearly |
| **Voice** | How you sound — tone, phrases, words you'd never use | Outputs sound like you wrote them, not a chatbot |

These four files live in the **Context** folder. They're the first thing the AI reads before generating anything.

---

## How to Fill Them In

You have three options. Pick whichever feels easiest:

### Option 1: Answer the questions (10 minutes per file)
Open each file and fill in the sections. They're written as prompts — just answer them like you're talking to a colleague.

### Option 2: Use the extraction skill (20 minutes total)
Open the chat panel and type:
```
/extract soul
```
The AI will ask you questions one at a time. Your answers go straight into the file. Do the same for `/extract audience`, `/extract offer`, and `/extract voice`.

### Option 3: Import what you already have (5 minutes)
If you have existing proposals, emails, website copy, or presentations, type:
```
/import
```
The AI will read your documents and pull out the relevant context. Faster than starting from scratch.

## What Happens Next

Once your four files have content, the vault starts working for you:

### Week 1 — Foundation
- Your four Context files move from "draft" to "active"
- The AI can now generate content that sounds like you
- Try: `/content LinkedIn post about [your topic]`
- Try: `/email cold outreach to [your audience]`

### Week 2 — First Decisions
- After a meeting or call, capture what happened:
  ```
  /follow-up Had a call with [prospect] — discussed [topic]
  ```
- Log strategic decisions as files in the **Decisions** folder with dates and reasoning
- The AI reads these before generating anything, so your outputs stay aligned with your strategy

### Week 3 — Research Starts Compounding
- Ask the AI to research something:
  ```
  /research [competitor name]
  /research [industry trend]
  ```
- Research files go into the **Research** folder
- When you ask for content later, the AI cross-references your research with your Context files
- Your LinkedIn posts now reference real data. Your proposals cite specific competitors. Your ads address real objections.

### Month 2 — The Compound Effect
- Your vault now has 4 Context files, 10+ Decisions, 5+ Research files
- Every output is better than the last because there's more context to draw from
- You notice: "This sounds like something I'd actually say"
- Try: `/campaign` to generate a full ad + email + post + landing page in one go

### Month 3 — Your Digital Twin
- The AI knows your contrarian beliefs, your signature phrases, your anti-positions
- It knows who your ideal buyer is and what they tried before finding you
- It knows your pricing, your guarantees, your delivery process
- Content generation takes seconds, not hours
- Your vault is now a competitive advantage no one can copy

## The Compound Loop

This is the engine:

```
You share expertise
    ↓
Context files get richer
    ↓
AI reads better context
    ↓
Outputs improve
    ↓
You refine what works
    ↓
Context files get richer (again)
```

Every conversation, every decision, every piece of research feeds back into the system. Your vault doesn't decay like a consultant's report sitting in a drawer. It compounds.

## Daily Use

You don't need to do anything complicated. Here's what most clients do:

**Morning (2 minutes)**
- Type `/brief` in the chat panel
- The AI shows you what changed overnight, what needs attention, and what to focus on

**After a meeting or call**
- Type `/follow-up [what was discussed]`
- Takes 30 seconds. Saves weeks of lost context.

**When you need content**
- Type `/content LinkedIn post about [topic]` or `/email sequence for [audience]`
- The AI reads your full vault before writing. No more "write me a post about leadership" with zero context.

**On WhatsApp**
- Message your Pocket Architect anytime — ask questions, request research, review drafts
- Every message is grounded in your vault
- Voice notes work too — just talk, and the AI extracts the context
