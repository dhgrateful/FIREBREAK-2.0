# FIREBREAK — Cold Call Scripts (Hormozi style)

Cold calls — they are NOT expecting it. Honest open, create curiosity, lead with THEIR
problem, go for a micro-commitment. Danny Hamm = Director of Business Development.
Fit Check booking link: https://link.powerinlocal.com/widget/booking/hQNQc6UdEgjUszNcEoj0

Two scripts per category:
- SCRIPT A — Danny calling direct: create curiosity, gauge fit, soft-book a 15-min demo (8-min Fit Check as the fallback).
- SCRIPT B — a setter calling: one job only — book an 8-minute Fit Check with Danny.

# PRIVATE INDUSTRY
Buyers: Fire Chief / brigade lead, EHS / Process Safety, Ops / Risk / Facilities.

## SCRIPT A — Danny direct
Open: "Hey [Name], this is Danny Hamm — I'll be straight with you, this is a cold call. You can hang up, or give me 30 seconds and then decide. Fair?"
Hook: "Appreciate it. I run business development for a new fire tool called FIREBREAK. Quick reason I called — at a place like [Company], when a fire starts near a process unit or tank farm, the first few minutes decide how bad it gets, and water or foam near live equipment isn't always the answer. We built something for exactly that gap: it fights fire with air — no water, no foam, no chemicals. It holds or pushes a flame front back on demand, mounted on equipment your crew already runs."
Fit question: "I'm not selling you anything on this call — I'm honestly just trying to figure out if it's even relevant to you. Who owns first-response and fire planning at your site — you, or someone on your team?"
Curiosity spike: "Here's the part that usually gets people — in a field test it moved a flame front about 6x faster than the control burn at just 55% power, with nothing but air. Most folks in your seat have never seen a fire tool that works without water."
Ask (demo): "It's honestly hard to picture over the phone — it clicks the second you see it move fire with no water. If it sounds even a little relevant, let's put 15 minutes on the calendar and I'll walk you through exactly how it deploys. Worst case, you've seen something brand-new in the space. Open to that?"
Fallback (8-min): "No problem — let's not even do a full demo yet. Let's do an 8-minute Fit Check: I tell you what it does, you tell me if it fits your site, and if it doesn't we both move on. Better later this week or early next?"
Objections:
- "Is this a sales call?" -> "Honestly? A soft one. I'm not asking you to buy — I'm asking for 8 minutes to see if it's worth your time. If it's not, I'll be the first to say so."
- "Just email me." -> "Happy to — but the reason I called is it's hard to get from an email; it lands when you see it move fire. What's your best email? And let's hold 8 minutes so it actually sticks."
- "We're not looking for anything." -> "Fair — most people I call aren't. But foam's getting regulated out and water doesn't work everywhere, and this is the first thing that holds a line on air alone. If that's ever going to matter at [Company], 8 minutes now beats a scramble later."
- "No time." -> "That's exactly why I kept it to 8 minutes. I'll make it the most useful 8 minutes of your week — deal?"
Voicemail: "Hey [Name], Danny Hamm with FIREBREAK. We built a new fire tool that holds and redirects a flame front using only air — no water, no foam, no chemicals — and it's turning heads with on-site brigades. Small ask: 8 minutes to see if it even fits [Company]. I'll text you too. Thanks [Name]."
