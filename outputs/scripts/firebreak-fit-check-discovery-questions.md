# FIREBREAK — 8-Minute Fit Check (Discovery Questions)

Goal in 8 minutes: figure out together whether FIREBREAK is worth a real demo. A
conversation, not an interrogation — listen 80%, talk 20%. Qualify the fit and book
the demo, or disqualify honestly.

## 1) Frame it (first 30s)
"Appreciate the 8 minutes. My only goal is to figure out together whether FIREBREAK's even worth a closer look for you — a few quick questions, the 60-second version of what it does, and if it's not a fit I'll tell you straight. Fair?"

## 2) Situation — what they protect & use today (pick 2)
- "What are you responsible for protecting, and what's your first line when a fire starts?"
- "What are you using today — water, foam, dozers, hand crews — and where does it fall short?"
- "When it comes to fire risk at [site/operation/community], what keeps you up at night?"

## 3) Problem — surface the pain (pick 1-2 by category)
- Private industry: first 5-10 minutes before mutual aid; where water/foam is a liability near process; where they are on the AFFF/foam transition.
- Airports: where they are in the AFFF phase-out; how they handle grass/brush fire along the field.
- Wildfire: how often holding line with no water access; how much loss risk is embers ahead of the front.
- Prescribed burns: acres not getting done because of the window; any containment close calls.
- HOA / WUI: insurance non-renewal / defensible-space pressure on common areas; who clears the greenbelts today.

## 4) Stakes — cost of the problem (pick 1)
- "If that went wrong — [unit fire spreads / escape / non-renewal / loss] — what does it actually cost you?"
- "What's the cost of living with it another season?"

## 5) Decision (pick 1-2)
- "If it looked like a fit after a demo, who else is part of that conversation?"
- "Any budget or timing window we should know about — fiscal year, fire season, renewal/audit date?"

## 6) The 60-second 'what it is'
"FIREBREAK fights fire with air — no water, no chemicals. [Category benefit.] Patented, Made in the USA, and in a field test it moved a flame front ~6x faster than the control burn at 55% output."

## 7) Fit + next step
- Fit: "Honestly, this sounds like a fit. It clicks when you see it move fire — let's grab 15-20 minutes where I walk you through how it deploys and we talk specifics for [site]. [day] or [day]?"
- Not yet (integrity close): "Based on what you're telling me, I don't want to waste your time — [honest read]. If [trigger] ever changes, I'll reach back out."

## Qualification cheat
GREEN: owns the ground/response · feels the pain · budget authority or access · timing trigger.
RED: info-gatherer, no authority · no pain · no timeline · "just send me stuff."
