# FIREBREAK — Setter Gatekeeper Script

Getting past the front desk to the decision-maker. Treat the gatekeeper as an ally,
never an obstacle. Warm, confident, brief, honest — and smile, they hear it.

**Golden rule — never hang up empty-handed.** Leave with at least ONE of: a transfer,
the right person's name, a direct line/extension, a working email, or the best callback time.

## Opening — when you HAVE the person's name (most of our list)
"Hi, could you put me through to [First Last], please? ... Thanks so much."
(Calm and confident — often that alone gets you through.)

## Opening — when you DON'T have a name (main line)
"Hi — quick one, maybe you can help me out. Who at [Company] would handle [ROLE]? ... Perfect — is [Name] available, or what's the best way to reach them?"

ROLE by category:
- Private industry -> fire response / EHS / process safety (plant fire chief or safety manager)
- Airports -> ARFF / airfield fire response (airport fire chief or director of public safety)
- Wildfire -> the fire chief, or the fuels / operations lead
- Prescribed burns -> burn boss, fire program manager, or land manager
- HOA / WUI -> the general manager, or community manager over common areas & maintenance

## Enlist them (the ally move)
"You'd honestly know this better than me — who's the right person to even ask whether something like this is a fit? I don't want to waste anyone's time, yours included."

## Gatekeeper objections
- "What's this regarding?" -> "Of course — it's about a new fire tool called FIREBREAK. I just need a couple of minutes with [Name] to see if it's even relevant to [Company]. Is he/she around?"
- "Are they expecting your call?" -> "No — first time reaching out, which is exactly why I'm keeping it short. If now's bad, when's a better time to catch [Name]?"
- "Is this a sales call / solicitation?" -> "Honestly, a soft intro, not a hard sell. I'm just finding out if it's worth 8 minutes of [Name]'s time. If it's not, they'll tell me and I'm out of your hair."
- "We don't take solicitations / send it to purchasing." -> "Understood — this isn't a quote, it's a new fire-safety tool I think [Name] would want to know exists. What's the best email to reach them, and who should I follow up with?"
- "Just email info@..." -> "Happy to — what's the email that actually lands with [Name or role]? And when's a good time to follow up so it doesn't sit in the general inbox?"
- "They're not available / in a meeting." -> "No problem — when's typically the best time to catch [Name], morning or afternoon? And is there a direct line or extension?"
- "I can take a message." -> "Appreciate it — let them know [Your Name] from FIREBREAK called about a new fire tool, and I'll try back [time]. What's the best direct number or email so it gets to them?"
- "Who are you again?" -> "[Your Name], with FIREBREAK — a new fire-safety tool. Just need two minutes with [Name]."

## If it routes to voicemail
Use the Setter voicemail from that category's script — short: who you are, "a new fire tool that works with air, no water or chemicals," setting up an 8-minute fit check with Danny, and you'll text too.

## Tone reminders
Slow down. Be warm. Use the gatekeeper's name if they give it. Thank them like they did you a favor — because they did. Gatekeepers open doors when you treat them like the key, not the lock.
