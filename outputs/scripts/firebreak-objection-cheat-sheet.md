# FIREBREAK — Objection Cheat-Sheet

Use: Acknowledge -> reframe -> back to the ask (8-minute Fit Check). Never argue; agree first, then redirect.

- "Not interested." -> "Fair — most people I call aren't, this is new. But foam's getting regulated out and water doesn't work everywhere, and FIREBREAK is the first thing that holds a line on air alone. If that ever matters at [Company], 8 minutes now beats a scramble later."
- "No budget / what's it cost?" -> "Not asking you to spend anything today — 8 minutes to see if it's a fit. The number to weigh it against is what one [unit fire/escape/non-renewal/loss] costs. Pricing comes on a demo. Fair?" (Never quote price cold.)
- "We already have a system / we're covered." -> "Good — this isn't a replacement for engines/foam/crews, it's a layer for what they can't cover: the first minutes, the ground water can't reach, the embers ahead of the line. 8 minutes to see where it fits?"
- "Send me an email." -> "Happy to — but it clicks when you see it move fire, not from an email. Best email? And let's hold 8 minutes so it actually lands."
- "No time." -> "Exactly why it's 8 minutes. Most useful 8 of your week or I never call again — deal?"
- "Not now / call me back." -> "Of course — when's genuinely better, this week or next? I'll lock an 8-minute slot so neither of us chases it."
- "I'm not the right person." -> "No problem — who at [Company] would look at first-response / fire tools? I'll reach out — thanks for pointing me right."
- "Too good to be true / does it work?" -> "Fair skepticism — I'd rather you see it. Patented, Made in USA, field test ~6x faster than the control burn at 55% output. That's the point of the 8 minutes — decide for yourself."
- "We've done it this way for years." -> "No doubt — this isn't about doing it wrong, it's about the gap the old way leaves: [first minutes / no-water ground / shrinking window / common-area fuel]. If that gap bites, 8 minutes now saves the bad day."
- "Is this a sales call?" -> "A soft one. Not asking you to buy — 8 minutes to see if it's worth your time. If not, I'll say so first."
- "Run it by the board / team." -> "Makes sense — the 8 minutes arms you with the facts for when it comes up. Want me to grab a slot?"
