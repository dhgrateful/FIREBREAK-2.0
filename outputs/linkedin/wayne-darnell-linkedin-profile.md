### About section (main)
I build tools for the people who fight fire.

FIREBREAK is a new kind of fire tool — it fights fire with air. No water. No foam. No chemicals.

It's a machine-directed air stream (our patented M-DAS system) that can impede or steer a flame front on demand — up to 156 MPH and 337,523 CFM — mounted on equipment crews already own and ready the moment it's offloaded. In field testing it moved a flame front nearly 6× faster than an adjacent control burn at just 55% of its output.

Why it matters: the tool you need most is often the one that works where water can't — no hydrant for miles, no engine access, live process units where water is a liability, or a firefighting foam supply that's being regulated away. FIREBREAK holds a line, redirects embers, and buys the first critical minutes — with nothing but air.

Who we serve:
• Wildfire & wildland fire agencies and contractors
• Prescribed burn and land-management teams
• Industrial sites with an on-site brigade — refineries, chemical, LNG, utilities
• Airport ARFF and fire departments
• WUI communities and HOAs protecting common-area fuel breaks

Made in the USA, powered by Panther Airboats. U.S. Patents 11,446,687 & 11,980,784.

Curious whether it fits what you protect? Grab an 8-minute Fit Check — no pitch, just a quick look at whether it's worth exploring. Link in Featured below, or reach out and I'll send it over.
